⏱️ Escalation Protocol

  • Pulse 1 (0-30s): Alert dispatched directly to your designated unit terminal with acoustic pulse.
  • Pulse 2 (30-60s): Secondary pulse chime if unacknowledged after 30s.
  • Pulse 3 (60-90s): Final attempt urgent siren trigger.
  • Automatic Admin Escalation (90s+): Alert auto-escalates to Admin Command and Designated Backup Chief Officer.

🛡️ Response Guidelines

1. Check the Prominent Location Banner at the top of the card for exact zone, room number, and GPS coordinates.

2. Tap Acknowledge Alert immediately to pause the 30-second escalation timer.

3. Tap Mark En Route when departing base station.

4. Tap Arrived on Scene to update student/staff reporter live status.

5. Verify area safety and tap Mark Resolved once the incident is fully handled.
